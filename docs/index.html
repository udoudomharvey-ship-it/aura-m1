What stays on this device

  • Video is never saved or uploadedCamera frames are processed in memory only.
  • This simulation page uses no camera or microphoneOn this hosted page everything runs in your browser from recorded data. In the installed app it is served from your own computer.
  • Nothing goes to your employer or a teamThere is no team view or manager report. It is a personal tool.
  • Voice is the only optional network useIf you turn it on, a short text request goes to the Anthropic API. Never video or body data.
